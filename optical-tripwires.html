How to navigate the 3D builder
  1. Choose Open 3D model for B or C.
  2. Drag one finger to orbit the camera. Pinch to zoom.
  3. Press Show exploded near the top of the 3D viewer to separate components.
  4. Press Block beam or Restore beam to change the symbolic visual state.
  5. Open the Components panel to select individual LEDs, resistors, mounts and wires. You can also hide or isolate parts.
  6. Use Export to save a model or a complete .3dv project for later revision.
Engineering accuracy and safety

These scenes do not simulate real photodiode sensitivity, amplifier gain, threshold, pinout, electrical load, IR range or power-bank behavior. The B amplifier does not have a verified final circuit; the salvaged 2N4401 manufacturer's lead order is unknown. The C demonstration applies to identified Adafruit-style IR modules with an active-LOW open-collector receiver. A different module requires its own datasheet and output measurements.

No physical test has been completed by this software. Enclosures, wires and mounts are approximate teaching geometry and not certified for 3D printing. Use rated low-voltage power, properly limited indicator LEDs, and no laser, buzzer, speaker or audible alarm.
