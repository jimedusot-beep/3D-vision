Quick setupNo laptop needed
  1. Use your HyperGear in White flashlight mode first, or use a properly powered red LED in Red mode.
  2. Open directly in Safari, tap Start camera, and hold the phone stationary.
  3. Start 6–12 inches away. Tap the bright spot in the preview to position the target circle.
  4. With the light shining, tap ARM AUTOMATIC DETECTION just once and keep it on while the app measures it.
  5. Without pressing another button, cover and uncover the light. The detector and counter should respond automatically. Then increase the distance gradually.
A red LED is not a laser: you may see only a bright red point rather than a visible line in air. One salvaged LED may not work at 6–15 feet. Avoid direct eye exposure to bright sources or using lasers.
Camera troubleshooting & advanced options

Wrong camera? Use front-camera button or select a specific camera below. Black video? Close other camera apps, tap Stop, reload the page in Safari and try again. Jumping readings? Keep the phone still, tap the LED precisely, block other red lights and recalibrate. iOS adjusts exposure automatically.
