# Living engineering terminology

- **Parametric CAD:** geometry controlled by explicit variables.
- **STL:** triangle-mesh file, typically for slicing.
- **Watertight:** mesh encloses a volume topologically.
- **Manifold:** locally valid mesh surface topology.
- **BOM:** bill of materials and quantities.
- **Datum:** coordinate or surface reference.
- **Interface:** defined mating boundary between modules.
- **Clearance:** space reserved between mating parts.
- **Tolerance:** permitted departure from nominal dimension.
- **Mounting pitch:** distance between fastener centers.
- **Nut trap:** captured cavity for a standard nut.
- **Load path:** route mechanical forces take through a structure.
- **Center of mass:** mass-weighted center of system.
- **Torque:** turning moment about a rotational axis.
- **DOF:** independent mechanical degree of freedom.
- **Stall current:** motor current when output is obstructed under specified conditions.
- **Thermal envelope:** space and cooling allowance for dissipated heat.
- **Design envelope:** provisional geometric volume reserved for a module.
