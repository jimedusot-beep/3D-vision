## Status
- 18 individual prototype STL meshes, not physically printed or fitted.
- Main editable master: `02_MASTER_CAD_AND_REGISTERS/HERBIE_V1_Master.scad`.
- Interactive GLB files are single-file display assemblies for 3D viewers.
- `06_PRIOR_CONCEPT_REFERENCE` contains earlier concept files, not replacements for the master.
- Some parts exceed preferred 180 × 180 mm print-bed bounds; check orientation and segmentation before print.
- A mesh being watertight does NOT prove hardware fit or mechanical strength.
