It does not include lips, teeth, velum, pharynx, tongue-muscle layers, minor glands or complete swallowing mechanics. It is a static model, not an articulation or bolus simulation.

Connect the oral structures to speech and swallowing

  1. Articulation: The tongue and mandible help change oral shape and constrictions for many speech sounds. Tongue shape and positions during individual phonemes are not modeled.
  2. Oral preparation: Saliva moistens food, supporting chewing and the oral stage of swallowing. Saliva travels through ducts that open into the mouth.
  3. Upper neck: The hyoid helps anchor muscles involved in tongue, jaw, and laryngeal movement. Do not infer actual muscle trajectories from this simplified surface model.

Study activity: Find both parotid glands and ducts, both submandibular glands and ducts, both sublingual glands, then identify the tongue, mandible and hyoid. Switch to Isolate selected to compare them. The selected 3D mesh highlights in bright turquoise.
