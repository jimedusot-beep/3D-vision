Anatomy study: evidence and limitations

Illustrative geometry, not validated medical anatomy. The ten objects are original Three.js primitives in arbitrary display units. Their relative positions, sizes, tissue layers and muscle paths have not undergone anatomical or clinical review. Do not use them for diagnosis, surgery, competency evaluation, or 3D printing.

What is supported by references?

The viewer's short written descriptions and its six recall questions cite the educational works below. The references provide context for text explanations only; none supplies or certifies the 3D meshes.

  • OpenStax, Anatomy & Physiology 2e, §22.1 — Organs and Structures of the Respiratory System
  • NCBI Bookshelf, Anatomy, Head and Neck: Larynx
  • NCBI Bookshelf, Anatomy, Head and Neck: Laryngeal Muscles

Clinical terminology, muscle actions and teaching prompts require independent review by a qualified anatomy or speech-language professional before this becomes an authoritative educational atlas.

Orientation and missing structures

The schematic uses the subject's left and right, not the viewer's. In an anterior-facing view the subject's left appears to the viewer's right. Rotation changes screen positions; rely on structure labels instead of screen location. The shapes and their alignment are still unvalidated.

The illustration excludes many structures, such as the epiglottis, false vocal folds, hyoid, cricothyroid and thyroarytenoid muscles, interarytenoids, nerves, and the detailed layers of the glottis. Their absence from the picture does not indicate absence from the body.

Privacy, accessibility and licensing

No personal coursework, patient records or proprietary medical meshes are included in the public viewer. The review questions do not store scores. WebGL failure leaves text-based learning usable. Automated desktop and emulated-phone checks do not replace real-device or assistive-technology evaluation.

Potential future anatomical models require file-by-file source, version, license, and attribution review before reuse. A viewer's open-source code license does not establish permission to redistribute its 3D assets.

Release status

This is a draft prototype, not a clinical tool. Anatomy accuracy review, genuine 3D model provenance, physical-device usability, and integration with the private H.E.R.B.I.E. educational library remain incomplete.
