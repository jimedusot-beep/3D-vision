Hearing, balance, and communication

  1. Outer ear: The pinna shapes incoming sound; it travels through the unmodeled ear canal toward the eardrum.
  2. Middle ear: Eardrum vibration is relayed mechanically by malleus, incus and stapes. The auditory/Eustachian tube supports pressure equalization, not the pathway of sound vibration.
  3. Inner ear: Cochlear sensory hair cells (not visible in this mesh) help transduce mechanical motion into neural signals. Cranial nerve VIII carries hearing and balance information to central pathways (not modeled). The vestibule contributes to balance, not sound perception.

Recall questions: What are the three ossicles in order? Which structure converts mechanical input into auditory signaling? What is the difference between the cochlea and the vestibule?
