Phonation: how the larynx works

These are static concept diagrams, not views of the 3D model or simulations of living tissue. They distinguish glottal positioning from the airflow-driven vibration that produces voice.

Vibration: airflow and pressure from exhalation interact with the layered vocal folds to generate oscillation. Pitch: fold length, tension and tissue properties help determine fundamental frequency; the cricothyroid contributes to lengthening and tensing.

The imported GLB depicts vocal ligaments, not full mucosa-covered vibrating vocal folds. No waveform, anatomical displacement, or clinical function is computed from the mesh.
