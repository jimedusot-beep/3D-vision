Seven SLP-relevant nerve checkpoints

Follow CN V, VII, the available vestibular branch of VIII, IX (text only), X, XI and XII. Selectable source meshes are highlighted when available. This is a functional study sequence, not a real nerve route or brainstem circuit.

Study connections and limits

  1. Articulation and oral control: CN V supports chewing and facial sensation, CN VII facial expression and lip control, and CN XII most tongue muscle movement.
  2. Swallowing and voice: CN IX sensory functions and CN X laryngeal/pharyngeal functions are important. CN IX is not present as a 3D structure in this dataset.
  3. Hearing: The displayed vestibular nerve is balance-related. Study the cochlea and complete auditory concepts in the separate hearing lab; do not treat this as a model of the cochlear nerve or the central auditory system.

References: NCBI cranial-nerve overview, NCBI anatomy of swallowing and ASHA dysphagia practice portal.
